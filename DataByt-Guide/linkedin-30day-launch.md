## PART 1 — CRACK THE LINKEDIN SYSTEM FIRST

### How the Algorithm Actually Works (Not What LinkedIn Tells You)

```
The algorithm has ONE goal:
Keep people on LinkedIn longer.

It rewards posts that generate COMMENTS.
Comments = people debating = people staying on LinkedIn.

Priority order:
1. Comments (worth 10x a like)
2. Reposts with thoughts (worth 5x)
3. Reactions (worth 1x)
4. Shares without comment (worth 0.5x)
5. Link clicks (PUNISHED — takes people off LinkedIn)
```

### The Golden Window

```
First 60-90 minutes after posting = make or break.

LinkedIn shows your post to 5-10% of your followers first.
If they engage → shows to more people → goes wide.
If they ignore → post dies. Nobody sees it.

What this means:
→ Post when your audience is ONLINE
→ Be ready to reply to EVERY comment in the first hour
→ Comment yourself on other people's posts BEFORE you post
   (warms up the algorithm to show YOUR post)
```

```
CEI only improves when you actually collect more efficiently.

Benchmarks:
→ Below 70%: Collections process is broken
→ 70-85%: Average, room to improve
```

**Hook:**
```
A simple method to predict next month's cash collections within 10%.

No software required. Takes 30 minutes.
```

**Body:**
```
Step 1: Pull your AR aging report right now.

Step 2: Apply these collection probability rates by bucket:

0-30 days overdue:   85% probability of collecting in 30 days
31-60 days overdue:  65% probability
61-90 days overdue:  40% probability
90+ days overdue:    20% probability
Under dispute:        0% (until resolved)

Step 3: Multiply each bucket total by its probability.

Step 4: Sum the results. That's your expected cash collections.

Example:
0-30d: $320,000 × 85% = $272,000
31-60d: $185,000 × 65% = $120,250
61-90d: $95,000 × 40%  = $38,000
90d+:   $42,000 × 20%  = $8,400
Expected: $438,650

Step 5: Compare to your minimum operating cash needs.
If expected < needs → you have a cash gap. Act now.

This model is more accurate than intuition.
More honest than optimism.
More useful than a board presentation with no forecast at all.
```
