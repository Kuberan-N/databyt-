The 10x math of a write-off
The Post
A write-off is the most expensive line in finance — and the quietest. When an invoice finally gets marked "uncollectable," it doesn't just cost the invoice amount. To replace $50K of written-off revenue at a 10% margin, you have to sell $500K more. That's the real math of bad debt: every dollar you fail to collect costs you ten dollars of new sales to make up for. Collecting what you're already owed isn't admin. It's the highest-margin revenue in your business.

🎓 BREAKDOWN

📐 StructureProvocative claim → the hidden math → the reframe ("highest-margin revenue").
💡 Steal thisTurn an abstract loss into a vivid multiplier ("$1 lost = $10 of new sales"). Math makes it stick.

Do early-payment discounts actually work?
The Post
Early-payment discounts feel smart. Usually they're not. Offering "2% off if you pay in 10 days" can work — but most companies hand the discount to customers who would've paid on time anyway, and the chronic late-payers ignore it completely. You end up paying your best customers to do what they already did, while your cash-flow problem (the late ones) is untouched. A discount is a blunt tool. Consistent, escalating follow-up is a precise one — and it doesn't cost you margin.
🎯 CFOs, finance leaders#CashFlow#Finance#CFO#WorkingCapital
🎓 BREAKDOWN
✍️ Hook — Myth-bust"Feel smart. Usually they're not." challenges a common tactic — readers want to know why.

📐 StructureMyth → why it fails → the wasted spend → blunt vs precise tool reframe.
💡 Steal thisContrast "blunt tool vs precise tool." It elegantly elevates your approach over the common one.
