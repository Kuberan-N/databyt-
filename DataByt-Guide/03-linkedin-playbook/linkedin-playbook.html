The weekly rhythm explained

  • Monday — open the week on a wound (pain). People are back at work feeling the problem.
  • Tuesday — your education carousel. Highest save-rate day. Builds authority.
  • Wednesday — founder journey. Mid-week humanizing. Makes them root for you.

  • Friday — poll or bold take. Engagement spike heading into the weekend.

Carousel rule: Only once a week, education only. They take longer to make and get bookmarked, not just liked. Don't try to do them daily — quality drops and you burn out.

When collections get out of hand, most companies do the expensive thing: hire. A collections agency takes 10–25% of what they recover — and often torches the customer relationship doing it.
