1 · What DataByt Does

DataByt is an AI-powered accounts receivable (AR) platform. It chases your overdue invoices automatically — so your team stops sending reminder emails by hand.

The full loop DataByt closes

StepWhat happens
1 ImportYour invoices come in via QuickBooks, Xero, CSV, or sample data
2 ScoreEvery overdue invoice is ranked by amount, age, and customer risk
3 EmailAI sends personalised dunning emails, escalating in tone (L1 → L2 → L3)
4 PayCustomers pay via your payment link (Stripe, bank, any URL you set)
5 DisputeDisputes pause collections automatically until resolved
6 AnalyseLive DSO, CEI, and collection trends — board-ready in one click

The promise: Connected and live in 48 hours. No IT team. Your finance team of 1–3 can run it.

Who runs it

  • CFO / VP Finance — watches the metrics, makes decisions
  • Controller / AR Manager — daily operator: reviews, marks paid, handles disputes
  • The AI Assistant — does the repetitive chasing automatically

2 · Getting Started

Step 1 — Log in

1
Go to databyt.in → click Sign in → enter your email and password. New here? Sign up creates your organisation automatically.

Step 2 — Get your data in (pick one)

A
Connect QuickBooks or Xero — Dashboard → Integrations → Connect → log in → Allow. Invoices import automatically. (~5 min)
B
Import a CSV — Integrations → Import CSV → download the template → fill it → upload. Required columns: invoice_number, customer_name, customer_email, amount, due_date.
C
Load sample data — Integrations → Load Sample Data. Instantly fills the dashboard with realistic demo invoices so you can explore everything. (Great for testing.)

Empty dashboard? That just means no data yet. The dashboard shows a guide card pointing you to the three options above. Pick one and the whole product comes alive.

Step 3 — Set your essentials (Settings)

  • Add your email signature (goes on every dunning email)
  • Set your payment link template so emails include a "Pay now" link
  • Confirm your collection rules (when L1/L2/L3 emails fire)

4 · AR Aging

The operational heart of DataByt. Every invoice, grouped by how overdue it is — and where you take action.

The aging buckets

BucketMeaning
CurrentNot yet due — healthy
1–30 daysRecently late — gentle reminders
31–60 daysGetting serious — firm follow-up
61–90 daysHigh risk — escalate
90+ daysCritical — may become bad debt

The invoice table — your 3 actions

Each invoice row (on the Action column) gives you three buttons:

1
Email — opens the AI email drafter. Pick escalation level (L1/L2/L3), the AI writes it, you review and send.
2
Pay link — copies a payment link for that invoice (if you've set a template in Settings). If not set, it links you to Settings to add one.
3
Mark Paid — the green button. Click it the moment a customer pays. The invoice updates instantly and stops further chasing.

Always Mark Paid when a payment lands. DataByt can't see your bank account — when you confirm a payment, click Mark Paid so it stops emailing a customer who already paid. Takes 5 seconds.

Use the filter tabs (All / Overdue / Open / Paid) and the search box to find invoices fast.

6 · Disputes

When a customer contests an invoice, collections should pause — not keep nagging. Disputes handle that.

How disputes work

1
File — from AR Aging, click the dispute icon on an invoice (or ask the AI Assistant). Pick a reason: incorrect amount, goods not received, duplicate, already paid, service not rendered, other.
2
Pause — collections automatically stop for that invoice while the dispute is open.
3
Resolve or reject — from the Disputes page. Resolving resumes collections; the invoice returns to its normal flow.

Why this matters: Chasing a customer who has a legitimate dispute destroys the relationship. Disputes keep you professional.

12 · The AI Assistant

The chat widget (bottom-right of every dashboard page). It doesn't just answer — it takes actions.

What you can ask

  • "Who owes me the most?" → ranked list of overdue customers
  • "How many overdue invoices do I have?" → live count
  • "What's my total AR outstanding?" → the number
  • "How much does [customer] owe?" → that customer's balance + breakdown
  • "When did we last email [customer]?" → communication history
  • "How do I connect QuickBooks?" → product help

What it can do (actions)

  • Send a reminder — "Send a reminder to [customer]" → it confirms, then sends
  • File a dispute — "File a dispute for INV-023" → it confirms reason, then files

It always confirms before acting. The Assistant will never send an email or file a dispute without asking you first.

The toolbar

  • Minimize (–) keeps your chat session alive in a small bar; Close (×) closes it
  • Escalate an issue — sends a flagged message to the DataByt team
  • Request a feature — submit an idea directly

13 · Your Daily Workflow

DataByt does the heavy lifting automatically. Your job is a 5-minute daily check.

The automatic part (no action needed)

  • Dunning emails go out Monday–Friday at 8am, escalating by age
  • Cooldown of 3 days minimum between emails to the same customer
  • Disputes pause collections automatically

Your 5-minute daily routine

1
Open the Dashboard — glance at DSO, CEI, Overdue %. Anything red?
2
Check your bank — any payments landed? Go to AR Aging → Mark Paid on those invoices.
3
Review Disputes — resolve or reject anything open.
4
Scan the Collections pipeline — any "Not Contacted" customers that need a manual nudge? Use the AI Assistant.

That's it. Five minutes a day. The system runs the other 23 hours and 55 minutes.

Weekly

  • Generate a Reports PDF for your records / the board
  • Review Analytics — is DSO trending down? Is CEI above 80%?

14 · FAQ & Troubleshooting

The dashboard is empty.

You have no invoice data yet. Go to Integrations and connect QuickBooks/Xero, import a CSV, or load sample data.

A customer paid but still shows overdue.

DataByt can't see your bank. Go to AR Aging → find the invoice → click Mark Paid. This is the one manual step (or reconnect your accounting integration so payments sync automatically).

Why does a new customer show "Not Contacted"?

Dunning emails run Mon–Fri at 8am. New overdue invoices wait for the next scheduled run. Nothing is broken — it's queued.

How do I stop emails to one customer?

Mark their invoices as paid, or file a dispute — both pause collections for that customer.

Can I customise the email tone?

Yes — set the customer's segment (Strategic / Standard / At-risk) in Customers, and adjust thresholds in Settings → Collection Rules.

The AI Assistant says "unavailable."

Usually a temporary connection issue — try again in a moment. If it persists, use the "Escalate an issue" button in the chat.

How do I get a board report?

Reports → Generate PDF Report. One click.

NEED HELP?

Use the AI Assistant's "Escalate an issue" button, or email support@databyt.in.
