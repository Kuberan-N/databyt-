## The Four Analytics Areas

### 1. AR Aging Dashboard

```
What it shows:
→ Every unpaid invoice
→ Sorted by days overdue
→ Colour coded: green (current) → yellow (mild) → red (severe)
→ Customer name, amount, invoice number, level (L1/L2/L3)
→ Last email sent date
→ Quick actions: Email, Mark Paid, Dispute

Think of it as: The live heartbeat of your AR.
```

---

### 2. DSO Trend

```
What it shows:
→ Your average DSO over the last 6 months
→ Going down = DataByt is working ✅
→ Going up = something needs attention ❌

Example:
Jan: 68 days
Feb: 62 days
Mar: 57 days
Apr: 51 days
May: 46 days   ← target getting close
Jun: 42 days   ← target reached

Each bar is one month. Trend line shows direction.
```

---

### 3. CEI Score (Collections Effectiveness Index)

```
Formula:
CEI = (Beginning AR + Credit Sales - Ending AR) / (Beginning AR + Credit Sales) × 100

Simple version:
→ Of all the money you could have collected, how much did you actually collect?

Example:
Could collect: ₹10,00,000
Actually collected: ₹8,70,000
CEI = 87%

Shown as a gauge dial in DataByt:
→ 0-60%:  Red (poor)
→ 60-80%: Orange (needs work)
→ 80-90%: Yellow (good)
→ 90%+:   Green (excellent)
```

---

### 4. Cash Flow Forecast

```
What it shows:
→ How much money is expected to arrive and when

Based on AR aging buckets:

0–30 days overdue:  ₹3,20,000 → likely arrives this week
31–60 days overdue: ₹1,85,000 → likely arrives this month
61–90 days overdue: ₹95,000   → uncertain, may need L3
90+ days overdue:   ₹42,000   → at risk, manual action needed

Shown as a bar chart so CFO can plan cash positions.
```

## Email Analytics

```
For every email DataByt sends, it tracks:

Sent        → email was delivered
Opened      → customer opened it (pixel tracking)
Clicked     → customer clicked the payment link
Bounced     → email address is invalid
Failed      → Resend could not deliver

In Collections page you see:
→ Open rate: 68% (industry average: 25%)
→ Click rate: 34%
→ Payment rate after email: 22%
```
