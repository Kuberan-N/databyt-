## WHO TO TARGET

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
- B2B company (sells to other businesses, not consumers)
- $5M–$50M annual revenue
- 20–200 employees
- Using QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, or Sage
- Has 1–3 people on the finance/AR team
- DSO > 45 days OR has complained about cash flow
- Industries: professional services, SaaS, manufacturing, distribution, staffing, logistics

**Who in the company to contact:**
- Primary: CFO, Finance Director, Controller
- Secondary: AP/AR Manager, Head of Finance

**Red flags (don't waste time):**
- Consumer business (B2C) — no AR collections problem
- Revenue under $1M — not enough AR volume to justify $3K/month
- Using SAP or Oracle — outside current integration set
- Government entity — payment cycles are fixed, dunning won't help

## DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (30 minutes)

**Goal:** Qualify the prospect, identify their DSO and AR process, anchor on the ROI, and book a demo.

### Opening (2 min)
> "Thanks for jumping on. I want to make sure this is useful for you, so let me ask a few questions first — then I'll show you exactly what DataByt does and you can decide if it's relevant. Sound good?"

### Discovery Questions (10 min)

Ask these in order. Listen for pain signals.

**1. What accounting software do you use?**
*(Qualifying — must be QB/Xero/NetSuite/Sage)*

**2. What's your current DSO, roughly?**
*(Anchor for ROI calculation later)*
- If they don't know: "How many days does it usually take from invoicing to getting paid?"
- If DSO < 30: "That's excellent. Is it consistently that low, or do you have months where it spikes?"

**3. Walk me through how your team currently follows up on overdue invoices.**
*(Listen for: manual emails, spreadsheets, inconsistency, fear of damaging relationships)*

**4. How many people on your team spend time on AR collections?**
*(Staff cost anchor — 60–70% of their time = $36K/year in wasted labor)*

**5. When a customer disputes an invoice, what's your current process?**
*(Listen for: it goes into email threads, nobody tracks it, it stalls for weeks)*

**6. Does your CFO have a real-time view of collection performance?**
*(Listen for: they pull Excel reports on Fridays, or they don't have one)*

### Transition to Demo (2 min)
> "Based on what you've told me — [DSO X days, manual process, Y people spending time on this] — let me show you exactly what DataByt would do for you. This will take about 12 minutes."

### Demo Flow (12 min)

1. **Show the connection** — "This is what connecting your QuickBooks looks like. OAuth, 5 minutes, nothing to install."
2. **Show AR aging** — "This is what we see after the first import. Every overdue invoice, ranked by priority. The AI scores them — amount, days overdue, customer history."
3. **Show a dunning email** — "This is what your customer receives. Their name, their invoice numbers, exact amount, a direct payment link. One email per customer covering all overdue invoices. Tone escalates based on how overdue."
4. **Show the payment portal** — "Customer clicks this link. No login. They see their invoice, click pay, done. 60 seconds."
5. **Show the dashboard** — "CFO sees DSO trending, CEI score, email performance, 6-month collection trend. This PDF button generates a board-ready report."

### ROI Calculation (3 min)
> "You mentioned your DSO is [X] days and revenue is [Y]. At a 30% DSO reduction — our average — you'd go from [X] to [X×0.7] days. That frees up [($Y ÷ 365) × (X × 0.3)] in cash. Our service costs $36K/year. The math is [multiple]× in Year 1."

Pull up databyt.in/roi calculator if they're visual.

### Close (3 min)

**If positive signals:**
> "Based on what you've shared, this looks like a strong fit. We can typically have you live within 48 hours of connecting your accounting software. The next step would be a 30-minute onboarding call where we connect the system and configure your email sequences. Want to do that this week?"

**If hesitant:**
> "What would you need to see to feel confident this is the right tool for your team?"

## OBJECTIONS — EXACT RESPONSES

### "We're too busy right now."
> "The setup is 48 hours and we handle everything. Your team doesn't need to do anything except connect QuickBooks. The whole point is to give your team time back — not add to their workload. What does your calendar look like next Tuesday for a 30-minute onboarding call?"

### "We need to think about it."
> "Of course. What's the main thing you want to think through? I'd rather address it now so you have what you need to decide."
*(Wait for the real objection to surface)*

### "We already have someone handling this manually."
> "That person is probably spending 15–20 hours a week on tasks DataByt handles automatically. After this goes live, they can focus on escalations, customer calls, disputes — the work that actually requires judgment. They become 3× more effective, not replaced."

### "We don't have budget."
> "I get that. Let me ask — what's your current DSO? [Calculate cash tied up]. That's budget you already have, it's just sitting in uncollected invoices. The $3,000/month typically comes out of the cash DataByt frees up, not from a new budget line."

### "What if our customers complain about automated emails?"
> "Good question. Two answers: first, the emails are personalized with their specific invoice data — customers regularly respond thinking it came from your AR team. Second, if any customer is sensitive enough that you don't want them on automated dunning, you can exclude them in 10 seconds."

### "Can we try before committing?"
> "We don't have a free trial, but it's month-to-month. There's no annual commitment. If we don't cut your DSO in 90 days, you walk with no penalty. The risk is $3,000 for one month — the potential upside is [calculate cash freed]."

### "We tried software like this before and it didn't work."
> "Tell me more — what did you use?" *(Listen)* "The most common failure mode is either: (1) generic reminder software that isn't actually connected to your real AR data, or (2) enterprise tools that took 6 months to implement and nobody used. DataByt connects directly to your QuickBooks/Xero and is live in 48 hours. What specifically failed before?"

## AFTER THE SALE — ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

1. Connect accounting software (OAuth) — 5 minutes with customer
2. Review first AR aging import — confirm data looks correct
3. Configure from email address (customer's domain, e.g., ar@theircompany.com)
4. Set unsubscribe/opt-out preferences
5. Review dunning sequence settings (L1/L2/L3 thresholds)
6. Exclude any "do not contact" customers
7. First dunning run — review with customer before it sends
8. Set up dashboard access for CFO
9. Schedule 30-day check-in to review DSO trend
