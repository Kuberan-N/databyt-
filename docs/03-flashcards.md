## ROUND 1: Core Concepts

**Q: What does AR stand for, and what does it mean in one sentence?**
A: Accounts Receivable. Money your business has earned but hasn't received yet from customers.

---

**Q: What does DSO stand for and what does a high DSO mean?**
A: Days Sales Outstanding. High DSO = customers take longer to pay = more cash stuck in unpaid invoices.

---

**Q: If a company has $1M AR and $10M annual revenue, what is their DSO?**
A: ($1M ÷ $10M) × 365 = **36.5 days**

---

**Q: What are the 3 dunning levels and when do they trigger?**
A:
- L1: Just overdue (< 10 days) — polite reminder
- L2: 10–29 days overdue — firm notice
- L3: 30+ days overdue — final notice

---

**Q: What is CEI and what does a score of 100 mean?**
A: Collections Effectiveness Index. A score of 100 means you collected every dollar you could possibly collect. 80+ is good. Below 70 is a serious problem.

---

**Q: Name the 4 accounting systems DataByt integrates with.**
A: QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Business Cloud.

---

**Q: What is dunning?**
A: The systematic, scheduled process of following up with customers to collect payment on overdue invoices.

---

**Q: What is bad debt?**
A: AR you'll never collect. Has to be written off as a loss. Industry average: 1–3% of revenue per year.

---

**Q: What does "Net 30" mean on an invoice?**
A: Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.

---

**Q: What is cash application?**
A: Matching an incoming payment to the correct invoice in your accounting system.

## ROUND 2: DataByt Product

**Q: What is DataByt's core promise in three numbers?**
A: 30% DSO reduction. 48 hours to go live. $3,000/month.

---

**Q: What happens when a customer disputes an invoice in DataByt?**
A: Collections immediately pause on that invoice. It enters a dispute workflow: file → investigate → resolve → collections resume.

---

**Q: Why does DataByt send one email per customer instead of one per invoice?**
A: One batched email covering all overdue invoices is less spammy, more professional, and more likely to get read than 5 separate emails.

---

**Q: What does the customer see when they click the payment link?**
A: A hosted payment portal (no login needed) showing their invoice details and a checkout button. They can pay in under 60 seconds.

---

**Q: What happens immediately when a customer pays?**
A: Dodo Payments sends a webhook. DataByt auto-matches the payment to the invoice, marks it paid, and stops all further dunning emails.

---

**Q: What is the 3-day cooldown rule?**
A: DataByt won't send another email to the same customer for 3 days after the last one. Prevents over-contacting.

---

**Q: What is CAN-SPAM compliance in DataByt?**
A: Every email has an unsubscribe link. If a customer unsubscribes, DataByt stores that flag and never emails them again.

---

**Q: What does DataByt's CFO dashboard show?**
A: Total AR, DSO trending, CEI gauge, email open/click rates, payment conversion, 6-month collections chart, top overdue customers, and a one-click PDF report button.
