# First 10 Customers Playbook
### Exactly how to get from 0 to 10 paying customers

---

## Why 10 Customers is the Only Goal Right Now

Not 100. Not "scale." Just 10.

Here's why 10 matters:
- **5 customers** gives you enough data to calculate a real DSO improvement number
- **10 customers** gives you a case study, a testimonial, and a defensible claim
- **10 customers** × $3,000/month = **$30,000 MRR** — real business
- **10 customers** paying for 90 days proves retention, not just activation

Everything else — LinkedIn, content, advertising, product features — is secondary until you have 10 paying customers.

## The 3 Fastest Paths to First Customers

### Path 1: Your Existing Network (Fastest — Week 1–2)

**Who to contact:** Anyone you know personally who is:
- A CFO, finance director, controller, or business owner
- At a B2B company ($3M–$50M revenue)
- Using QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, or Sage

**Your ask is NOT a sales pitch. It is:**
> "I built something new. Can I show it to you and get your honest feedback? 30 minutes, no commitment."

This works because:
- They'll do it as a favour
- Once they see it, the pitch happens naturally
- If it's not for them, they'll refer you to someone it IS for

**Action:** Write down every person you know in finance or who runs a B2B business. Message them today. Aim for 20 outreach messages this week.

**Template:**
> "Hey [Name], I've been building something in AR automation and I'd love your honest reaction — you'd be the kind of person who'd tell me if it's actually useful or just a nice idea. 30-minute call this week? No pitch, just feedback."

---

### Path 2: LinkedIn Outreach (Week 1–4)

**Who to target:**
- Job title: CFO, Finance Director, Controller, VP Finance, Head of Finance
- Company size: 20–200 employees (filter: 11–50, 51–200)
- Industry: Professional services, SaaS, manufacturing, staffing, distribution
- Using QuickBooks or Xero (look for this in their company description or posts)

**How to find them:**
1. LinkedIn Sales Navigator (if you have it) — set filters above
2. Regular LinkedIn search — search "CFO" + industry + city
3. LinkedIn company search — find companies in your target profile, then find the finance lead

**Outreach message (connection request + message):**
> "Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] does [industry] — do you manage AR collections manually or do you have an automated system for that? Building something for finance teams like yours and curious if it's a real pain point at your scale."

**Follow-up after accepting:**
> "Thanks for connecting. Quick context — I built DataByt, which automates the full AR collections loop (AI dunning + payment links + dispute management) for QuickBooks/Xero users. Cuts DSO by ~30%, live in 48 hours. Worth a 20-minute call to see if it's relevant?"

**Volume target:** 20–30 outreach messages per day. Expect 10–15% response rate, 3–5% book a call.

### Path 3: Accountants & Bookkeepers as Channel Partners (Week 2–6)

This is the highest-leverage path that most founders miss.

**The insight:** Accountants and bookkeepers manage QuickBooks/Xero for 10–50 client businesses. If one accountant becomes a DataByt partner, they can refer all their clients.

**Who to contact:**
- Small accounting firms (2–20 people)
- Bookkeeping practices that specialize in QuickBooks or Xero
- Fractional CFO services
- Virtual CFO providers

**Your pitch to accountants:**
> "I built DataByt — it automates AR collections for your clients' QuickBooks/Xero data. When their DSO drops, they have more cash, they're happier with their accountant, and they stay longer. I'm looking for 3–5 accounting practices to partner with early. In exchange: referral fee, white-label option, or priority support. Worth a call?"

**Why accountants say yes:**
- Their clients complain about cash flow → they don't have a good answer
- DataByt makes their clients' businesses healthier → better retention for them
- Referral fee is revenue with zero extra work

**Referral fee structure to offer:**
- $500 one-time per referred customer who activates
- OR first month free for referred customers (accountant gets credit)

**Where to find them:**
- LinkedIn search "QuickBooks ProAdvisor" or "Xero Partner"
- Xero App Marketplace — find accounting practices listed there
- QuickBooks ProAdvisor directory
- Local business groups on Facebook/LinkedIn

## How to Run the First Demo (Step by Step)

**Before the call:**
- Look up the company on LinkedIn — revenue estimate, industry, number of employees
- Find their accounting software if you can (often mentioned in job postings or their accountant's website)
- Estimate their DSO if possible (professional services = likely 45–70 days)

**Opening (don't pitch yet):**
> "Before I show you anything, let me ask a couple of questions to make sure this is actually useful for you. What accounting software do you use? And roughly, how many days does it take from invoicing a client to getting paid?"

**The pivot to demo:**
> "Based on what you've told me — [their DSO, manual process] — let me show you what DataByt would do specifically for your setup."

**After the demo, close directly:**
> "Does this seem like something your team would actually use? Because we can have you live in 48 hours."

**If they say yes:** Book the onboarding call before you hang up.
**If they say "let me think about it":** Ask "What's the main thing you want to think through?" — surface the real objection.

For every new customer, record this **before they go live:**

| Revenue (annual) | $X |

## Early Pricing Strategy

**Recommended for first 5 customers:**
- Waive the $5,000 setup fee in exchange for:
  - Agreeing to be a reference customer
  - Sharing before/after DSO data at 90 days
  - 15-minute testimonial call (recorded, with permission)

**Do not discount the monthly fee.** $3,000/month is already aggressive for the value. Discounting it sets wrong expectations.

**Alternative:** Offer "pilot pricing" of $1,500/month for first 3 months, then $3,000/month. This reduces risk for the customer and gets you 3 months of data fast.

## The One Question to Ask Every Customer

At the 30-day mark, ask this exact question:

> "On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to recommend DataByt to a finance director you know?"

- 9–10: Ask for a referral immediately. "Who's one CFO or finance director you know who would benefit from this?"
- 7–8: Ask what would make it a 9. Fix that thing.
- Below 7: Emergency — find out what's wrong and fix it before they churn.

## Common Reasons First Customers Don't Convert (And How to Fix Them)

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| "The emails don't sound like us" | Set up a custom email tone review session. Show them how to adjust L1/L2/L3 tone. |
| "Some customers are complaining" | Add those customers to the exclusion list immediately. Show them the opt-out was handled. |
| "I don't see DSO improvement yet" | DSO takes 30–60 days to visibly improve. Show them the leading indicators: emails sent, open rate, payment link clicks. |
| "The dashboard is confusing" | Onboarding call — walk them through every metric personally. Create a 1-page "what to check every Monday" guide. |
| "We're too busy to set it up" | Do it for them. Ask for QuickBooks access, connect it yourself, run the first setup. Remove every barrier. |

## The Referral Ask (Don't Skip This)

Every happy customer knows 3–5 other CFOs or finance directors.

After their first successful dunning run (usually week 2–3), say:

> "I'm glad it's working well. We're still early and focused on getting the right customers on board — is there anyone you know who might benefit from this? Even just an introduction, no pressure on them."

Most people will give you one name if the product is working. One name = one more potential customer at zero acquisition cost.
