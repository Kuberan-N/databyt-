# Industry Verticals — Where to Focus First
### Not all industries have the same AR pain. Here's where DataByt wins fastest.

---

## Tier 1 — Go Here First (Highest Pain, Fastest Win)

### Professional Services
**Who:** Consulting firms, law firms, accounting firms, marketing agencies, IT services, staffing agencies.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- Sell time/expertise, get paid weeks later
- Clients often dispute scope or hours → lots of invoice disputes
- Senior staff (partners, directors) hate chasing clients for money
- Revenue is lumpy — one late invoice = real cash flow strain
- DSO typically 45–75 days

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "Your partners are too senior to chase invoices. Your AR team is doing it manually. We automate the full process — one AI email per client, payment link included — so your team focuses on billable work."

**DSO benchmark:** 42–55 days average | Target with DataByt: 30–38 days

**Best company size:** 20–150 employees, $3M–$30M revenue

**Keywords to use in outreach:** "billable hours," "client billing," "invoice aging," "slow-paying clients," "collections"

---

### B2B SaaS / Tech Companies
**Who:** Software companies that invoice on net terms (not subscription-only). Implementation fees, professional services, enterprise contracts.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- Enterprise SaaS contracts are huge — one unpaid invoice = massive cash flow hit
- Finance teams are small (1–2 people doing everything)
- CTOs and engineering leaders, not finance pros — AR isn't their strength
- Net 30–60 is standard, but enterprises pay at Net 60–90+

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "You're running a modern SaaS business but collecting invoices like it's 2005. Let the AI handle follow-up while your team focuses on the product."

**DSO benchmark:** 35–60 days | Target with DataByt: 25–42 days

---

### Marketing & Creative Agencies
**Who:** Digital marketing agencies, design studios, PR firms, video production companies.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- Project-based billing = lumpy revenue
- Clients often dispute deliverables → lots of disputes
- Agency owners hate the money conversation with clients
- Cash flow is the #1 reason agencies fail
- DSO 45–75+ days is common

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "You're great at client work. You hate chasing invoices. DataByt does the chasing — politely, automatically, with a payment link — so you never have an awkward money conversation again."

---

## Tier 2 — Strong Market (Slightly Longer Sales Cycle)

### Light Manufacturing & Distribution
**Who:** Small manufacturers, wholesale distributors, building materials companies.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- High invoice volumes (hundreds of invoices/month)
- Customers on Net 30–60 terms, often pay at Net 45–90
- Thin margins — late payment is an existential problem
- AR team is often 1–2 people managing hundreds of accounts
- DSO 50–75 days common

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "You're managing 200+ invoices a month with 2 people. One consolidated AI email per customer, a payment link, and a dispute portal means your AR team stops firefighting and starts managing by exception."

**Special consideration:** These companies often want to know about check payments. Make sure to clarify that Dodo Payments supports multiple payment methods including ACH/bank transfer.

---

### Logistics & Freight
**Who:** Trucking companies, freight brokers, 3PL providers.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- High volume, low margin industry
- Customers regularly short-pay (deduct accessorial charges)
- DSO 45–75+ days
- Short-pay and dispute management is a major pain point

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "Short-pay disputes sit in email threads for weeks. DataByt's dispute portal captures the reason, pauses collections on that invoice, and tracks it to resolution — so nothing falls through the cracks."

---

### Healthcare Services (Non-Insurance B2B)
**Who:** Healthcare staffing agencies, medical equipment suppliers, lab services selling to hospitals, dental supply companies.

**Why the AR pain is real:**
- Hospitals are notoriously slow payers (Net 60–90)
- High invoice values
- Complex billing, lots of disputes
- DSO 60–90+ days

**DataByt's pitch:**
> "Hospitals pay on their schedule, not yours. DataByt's systematic dunning doesn't let invoices age silently — it follows up every 3 days, escalates automatically, and gives your CFO a real-time view of what's collectible."

---

## Tier 3 — Harder (More Education Required)

### Construction
**Why it's harder:**
- Payment is often tied to project milestones and retainage (% held until project complete)
- Disputes are common and complex (liens, change orders, defect claims)
- DSO 70–100+ days, often by design
- May need more education on how DataByt handles these specific scenarios

**Still a fit for:** Subcontractors and specialty contractors who invoice GCs on standard commercial terms.

---

### Wholesale Retail / FMCG Distribution
**Why it's harder:**
- Large retailers (Walmart, Target) dictate their own payment terms and systems
- EDI compliance requirements
- Chargebacks and deductions are extremely common
- Needs more sophisticated dispute management than current DataByt build
