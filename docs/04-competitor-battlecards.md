# Competitor Battlecards
### How to position DataByt against every competitor you'll encounter

---

## 1. HighRadius

**What they are:** The enterprise gold standard for AR automation. Used by Fortune 500 companies.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2006 | Valuation: $3.1B
- Clients: Uber, Danone, Pepsi, Coca-Cola
- Pricing: $100,000–$500,000+/year
- Setup: 6–12 months with dedicated implementation team
- Target: Companies with $500M+ revenue

**Why your customers don't use them:**
They've never even had a sales call from HighRadius. The minimum contract is more than their entire AR team's annual salary.

**When a prospect mentions HighRadius:**
> "HighRadius is designed for Fortune 500 companies. Their minimum contract starts at $100K/year with a 6–12 month implementation. We're built specifically for the $3M–$100M market — same AI-driven outcome, live in 48 hours, at 10% of the cost."

**DataByt win factors:** Price (10× cheaper), speed (48 hrs vs. 12 months), no IT team needed.

---

## 2. Billtrust

**What they are:** AR automation focused on invoicing, cash application, and payment portals. Taken private by EQT at $1.7B in 2022.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2001 | Private equity-owned
- Pricing: $20,000–$60,000/year
- Setup: 3–6 months
- Target: Mid-market to enterprise, manufacturing and distribution heavy

**Their weakness:**
Strong on invoice delivery and cash application. Weaker on AI-driven collections/dunning automation. Legacy product feel.

**When a prospect mentions Billtrust:**
> "Billtrust is primarily an invoicing and payment network. It's $20K–$60K/year and takes 3–6 months to implement. We do the collections automation that Billtrust doesn't — AI dunning, payment links, dispute management — live in 48 hours at $3K/month."

---

## 3. Versapay

**What they are:** Collaborative AR platform — both buyer and seller use the platform for invoice management and payment.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2006 | Private
- Pricing: $50,000–$150,000+/year
- Target: Companies with $50M–$1B revenue in distribution, manufacturing, healthcare

**Their angle:**
"Collaborative AR" — gets the buyer to use a portal too, reducing disputes. Novel idea, but requires buyer adoption.

**Their weakness:**
Buyer-side adoption is difficult. Expensive. Not QuickBooks/Xero native. Overkill for sub-$50M companies.

**When a prospect mentions Versapay:**
> "Versapay requires your customers to also use their platform — that's a hard ask. We work with your customers through standard email and a payment link. No buyer enrollment. $3K/month vs. their $50K+/year."

---

## 4. Tesorio

**What they are:** AR automation + cash flow forecasting, funded by Y Combinator and Andreessen Horowitz.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2015 | $19.2M raised
- Pricing: $25,000–$120,000+/year
- Target: $10M–$200M revenue — closest to DataByt's segment
- Key differentiator: Cash flow forecasting AI

**Their weakness:**
Still expensive for the lower end of mid-market. Implementation is not instant. No QuickBooks-native self-serve.

**When a prospect mentions Tesorio:**
> "Tesorio is great if you need cash flow forecasting at $50K+/year. We're focused purely on collections — getting your invoices paid faster — at $3K/month, live in 48 hours. Much faster time to value."

---

## 5. Chaser

**What they are:** UK-based AR chasing tool, popular with Xero users. Simple, automated payment reminders.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2014 | Series A funded
- Pricing: £~$200–$700/month depending on plan
- Target: Small businesses using Xero/QuickBooks

**Their weakness:**
Simpler product. No AI prioritization. No payment portal. No dispute management. No CEI analytics. Positioned at SMB, not mid-market.

**When a prospect uses Chaser:**
> "Chaser is a good SMB reminder tool. For your scale [$X revenue, $Y AR], you need AI scoring to prioritize who to chase first, a dispute management workflow so collections don't stall, and a live CEI dashboard for board reporting. DataByt covers all of that."

---

## 6. Gaviti

**What they are:** Israeli startup focused on AR collections automation, backed by venture capital.

**The facts:**
- Founded: 2019 | $11.5M raised
- Target: Mid-market collections teams
- Pricing: Not public, but roughly mid-range

**Their weakness:**
Smaller brand, less proven in the US market. Weaker QuickBooks/Xero native integrations. No payment portal built-in.

---

## 7. Paidnice

**What they are:** Simple late payment automation tool integrated with Xero. Adds late fees, sends reminders.

**The facts:**
- Focus: Xero-only. Very simple.
- Pricing: ~$50–$200/month

**Not really a competitor:** Too simple for mid-market. No AI, no analytics, no dispute management.

---

## 8. QuickBooks / Xero Built-In Reminders

**The built-in tools:**
Both QuickBooks and Xero have basic invoice reminder functionality. You can set up automatic reminder emails.

**Why they're not the same as DataByt:**

| Feature | QB/Xero Built-in | DataByt |
|---|---|---|
| Personalization | Generic template, name only | AI-written with invoice details, amounts, payment history |
| Prioritization | None — sends to everyone | AI scores highest-risk first |
| Payment link | Basic (QB Pay / Xero Pay) | Dodo Payments hosted checkout, no login |
| Dispute management | None | Full workflow (pause → investigate → resolve) |
| CEI dashboard | None | Live gauge with email, payment, velocity metrics |
| Multi-ERP | N/A | QB + Xero + NetSuite + Sage |
| Board PDF report | None | One click |

**When a prospect says "we just use QuickBooks reminders":**
> "QuickBooks reminders are a starting point. They don't prioritize by risk, they don't adapt tone based on days overdue, and they don't give you a dashboard showing which customers are your biggest collection risks. It's the difference between a smoke alarm and a fire suppression system."
